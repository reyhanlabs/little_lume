👩‍🏫 For the Teacher

This is a student-facing play experience for LittleLume English — "My Body Part 1." Project it on a screen and let children take turns tapping, or guide them as a class. Each mini-game restores one of Lumi's glowing lights. Sound is read aloud by the browser — turn your volume up!

Covers: body-part vocabulary, "This is my ___," letters C/D & their sounds, and a movement chant — inside one continuous story.
