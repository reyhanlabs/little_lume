🎯 Today's Mission

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, I can do these things!

🙋
I can point to and say new body parts: arms, legs, fingers, toes, feet, and tummy.
🏃
I can move my body when I hear "Jump!", "Run!", "Walk!", or "Wave!".
💬
I can say "I have two ___." and "I can ___." about myself.
🔤
I can hear and copy the sounds E and F.
Today's Big Question

"What can YOUR body DO?" 🌈

Lumi says: Point to each icon and say the goal out loud together as a class before we start!

5-Minute Home Game

👪 Parent: Point to a body part and ask "What is this?"
🧒 Child: "This is my tummy." / "I have two arms." / "I have two legs."

👪 Parent: Ask "What can you do?" and act it out together.
🧒 Child: "I can jump!" / "I can run!" / "I can wave!"

📌 For Parents: No writing needed at home yet — just point, move, say, and smile together. 3–5 minutes a day is perfect for this age. 💛
