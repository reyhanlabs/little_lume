This is a shortened, story-driven My Body — Part 2. A little Shadow Monster steals Lumi's glow while she sleeps, and children help bring it back through seven quick steps: Story, Vocabulary, one Game, TPR movement, Speaking practice, a quick memory Review, and a Reward finale.

Words covered: arms, legs, feet, toes, tummy, shoulders, knees, body. Pattern: "This is my ___." / "I have ___." Designed to run about 20–25 minutes.
