GRAMMAR FOCUS

My Name 🙋

We use "Hello, my name is..." to tell our name.

👧
Hello, my name is Lily.

A short way to say it: "I am Lily."

Teacher Tip: Focus practice time on "Hello, my name is..." — mention "I am..." briefly as a bonus, not a full drill.
