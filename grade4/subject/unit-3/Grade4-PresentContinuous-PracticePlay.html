Teacher Notes: This is the practice companion — use it AFTER the "Present Continuous Lesson Book." It does not re-teach the grammar; it only reviews briefly and then jumps into games. Vocabulary here (cry, laugh, drive, fly a kite, etc.) is intentionally different from the Lesson Book's vocabulary list so students get fresh practice. Missions unlock one at a time. Estimated total time: 45-60 minutes.
Quick Review

📌 Remember this...

am / is / are + verb-ing = happening NOW
I am talking. He is smiling. They are watching.
