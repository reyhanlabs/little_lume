Before You Start

How to Use This Book

A quick guide for students and teachers before the speaking test.

🔊 For Students

Tap the speaker icon to hear how each word or sentence sounds.
Repeat out loud three times after listening.
Practise the dialogues with a partner or your teacher.
Try the Mock Oral Exam page as many times as you like.

⏱ Suggested Time

Eid al-Fitr & Eid al-Adha — 20 minutes
Cycling topics (4 pages) — 30 minutes
Mock Exam & Review Game — 15 minutes
Teacher Note

This book is built for oral exam preparation, not new grammar teaching — students should already know basic sentence patterns. Focus on pronunciation, confidence, and complete-sentence answers.
