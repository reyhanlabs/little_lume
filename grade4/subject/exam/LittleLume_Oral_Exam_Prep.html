✏️ Grammar: Types of Sentences

There are 4 types of sentences. Listen and repeat each one!

Declarative — tells something. Ends with a period (.)

Example: It is now rainy season.

Exclamatory — shows strong feeling. Ends with (!)

Example: That was amazing!

Imperative — tells someone to do something. Ends with (.)

Example: Please open the door.

Interrogative — asks a question. Ends with (?)

Example: May I go to the bathroom?

📖 Reading: Eid-ul-Adha

Eid-al-Adha is a religious holiday celebrated by Muslims worldwide to remember Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son Ismael for Allah. It is one of the two Eid festivals that Muslims celebrate.

Eid al-Adha is four days long and starts on the 10th day of the month of DhulHijja of the lunar Islamic calendar. This is the day after the pilgrims in Hajj go down from Mount Arafat. It happens approximately 70 days after the end of the month of Ramadan.

It is the day when many acts of worship are combined, such as reciting takbeerat, praying as a large community, and offering the udhiya (the sacrificial animal).

🗣️ Speaking Practice — Answer out loud:

1. What does Eid-ul-Adha remind Muslims of?
Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son Ismael for Allah.
2. How many days long is Eid al-Adha?
Four days long.
3. When does it start?
On the 10th day of the month of DhulHijja.
4. What is the "udhiya"?
The sacrificial animal.
5. How many days after Ramadan does it happen?
Approximately 70 days.

💬 Conversation: Where will you pray on Eid-ul-Fitr?

Akhlas:As-Salaamu'alaikum warahmatullaah.
Rafi:Wa'alaikumsalaam warahmatullaah.
Akhlas:Today is our last day of fasting in Ramadan.
Rafi:Alhamdulillah, we will complete our fasting today.
Akhlas:Where will you go for Eid-ul-Fitr prayer?
Rafi:My family goes to the prayer ground for Eid-ul-Fitr prayer.
Akhlas:Why at the prayer ground?
Rafi:Because the Prophet Muhammad said it's best to pray at the ground.

🗣️ Role Swap: First read as Akhlas, then switch and read as Rafi. Then try answering: "Where will YOU go for Eid-ul-Fitr prayer?" in your own words.

🎙️ Presentation: What I Do on Eid

Use these points to build your own spoken presentation.

1. Before Eid prayer: What do you do to get ready? (e.g. take a bath, wear new clothes, brush teeth)

2. During the Eid sermon: What do you do while listening?

3. After Eid prayer: What are your family traditions? (e.g. visiting family, offering sacrifice, sharing food)

🗣️ Speaking Practice: Say one full sentence for each point above. Example:

"Before Eid prayer, I take a bath and wear new clothes."

🏡 Tell Your Friend: Eid-ul-Fitr at My Home Town

As-Salaamu'alaikum warahmatullaah wabarakaatuh.

Brothers and sisters, Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha are the happiest days for me. My family and I perform Tarawih or night prayers every night. A week before Eid-ul-Fitr, my family goes to our home town.

I spent my Eid-ul-Fitr holiday together with all of my extended family. I visited my uncles' and aunts' houses and the houses of other relatives. I felt very happy and enjoyed spending my days there. They are some of the greatest days for our big family.

Thank you. Was-Salaamu'alaikum warahmatullaah wabarakaatuh.

🗣️ Your Turn: Practice telling your own short speech about Eid using this pattern: Greeting → Where you go → Who you visit → How you feel → Thank you.

🚴 Reading: Benefits of Cycling

There are many health benefits that are associated with cycling. They are:

  1. Cycling is one of the easiest ways to exercise
  2. Cycling builds strength and muscle tone
  3. Cycling burns calories
  4. Cycling improves heart health
  5. Cycling improves coordination
  6. Cycling reduces stress

🗣️ Speaking Practice — Answer out loud:

1. Does cycling benefit human beings? Why?
Yes, because it builds strength, burns calories, and improves heart health.
2. Choose ONE benefit and explain it in your own words.
Cycling reduces stress because it helps me relax and feel happy when I ride outside.
3. Why do you think cycling improves coordination?
Because you need to balance, steer, and pedal at the same time.

💬 Conversation: Where do you go for cycling?

Rafi:As-Salaamu'alaikum warahmatullaah.
Akhlas:Wa'alaikumsalaam warahmatullaah.
Rafi:Do you usually go cycling on weekend?
Akhlas:Yes, I do. I go with my family.
Rafi:Where do you go cycling?
Akhlas:I go around my housing complex. How about you?
Rafi:I used to go around the city.
Akhlas:Did you feel happy?
Rafi:Yes, I did. I had wonderful times!
Akhlas:I did too. Cycling makes us healthy.

🗣️ Role Swap: Read as Rafi, then swap to Akhlas. Then answer for yourself: "Do you usually go cycling on weekend? Where do you go?"
