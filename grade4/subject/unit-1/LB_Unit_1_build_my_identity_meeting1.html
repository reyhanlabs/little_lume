👋 Hello, Let's Meet!
Section 1 — Warm Up
👩‍🏫
Hi friends! I am so happy to see you today! Before we start, let's think about YOU. You don't need to write anything yet — just think in your head! 💭
🙋What's your name?
🎂How old are you?
🏠Where do you live?
🤫 Just think about your answers... we will use them on the next page!
⏱️
Estimated Time5 minutes
📋
Classroom Instructions
Sing a short "Hello" chant. Ask the three warm-up questions to 2-3 volunteers in Indonesian first, then model the English answer. Do not correct mistakes yet — this is just a thinking warm-up.
🤸
TPR
Wave both hands high for "Hello!". Point to self and smile when thinking of the answers.

🧩 Grammar Time
Section 4 — Three easy patterns
Pattern 1: I am ...
We use this to say our name.
I am Mia. 🙋‍♀️
I am Rafi. 🙋‍♂️
Pattern 2: I am ... years old.
We use this to say our age.
I am 7 years old. 🎂
I am 6 years old. 🎂
Pattern 3: I live in ...
We use this to say our city.
I live in Bekasi. 🏠
I live in Jakarta. 🏠
Pattern 4: What is your name? → My name is ...
We use this to ask and answer about a name.
A: What is your name? 🙋
B: My name is Dito. 😊
Pattern 5: How old are you? → I am ... years old.
We use this to ask and answer about age.
A: How old are you? 🎂
B: I am 9 years old. 😊
Pattern 6: Where do you live? → I live in ...
We use this to ask and answer about a city.
A: Where do you live? 🏠
B: I live in Surabaya. 😊
⏱️
Estimated Time14 minutes
📋
Classroom Instructions
Introduce one pattern at a time. Say the example sentences aloud, then invite 2-3 students to say their own version. For Patterns 4-6, pair students up so one asks the question and the other answers, then switch roles.
🤸
TPR
Students point to their partner on the question, then point to themselves on the answer.

Speaking Challenges — Move Around and Talk!
🔍
Find a friend who is 7 years old.
Ask "How old are you?" to your friends until you find one!
🙋
Ask 3 friends their names.
Say "What is your name?" to 3 different classmates.
🤝
Introduce your partner.
Say "This is [name]. He/She is [age] years old. He/She is [feeling]."

Estimated Time10 minutes
📋
Classroom Instructions
