Welcome back, helper! ✨

Because you helped Bobo, the Magic Forest is bright again! But now the forest needs your help to stay healthy...

Bobo says: "I know all my body parts now, thanks to you! But I feel wobbly... I forgot how to USE them and take care of my body!" Just then, a Health Crystal shatters into glowing pieces across Healthy Forest! This time, let's not just name body parts — let's wash, move, and take care of them like a real Healthy Hero.

🧭 Mission Briefing

Collect all 6 Health Crystals by using your body parts in real life: washing, moving, jumping, and staying healthy. Ready to level up?

Mission 1 · Quick Review + Warm-Up

Before we learn something new, let's warm up with what you already know! Then Healthy Forest will teach us two brand-new words: shoulders and knees.

🍃 Flip the leaves — find SHOULDERS and KNEES!

Mission 2 · Listening & Speaking

Bobo wants to practice talking about his body — and there's a Sight Word Cloud and a Speed Challenge waiting too!

Mission 3 · Health Crystal Cave (Phonics)

Deep in the Crystal Cave, glowing letters float in the dark. Let's review short vowels a, e, i to light the way!

Mission 4 · Reading

Look! Bobo's Healthy Journal is here! Let's read about how Bobo stays healthy.

I wash my hands.
I brush my teeth.
My body is clean.
My legs are strong.
I can run and jump.
I smile every day. I am healthy!

Let's check! 📖

Mission 5 · Writing

Now it's your turn to write! Describe your own face using my — just like Bobo did in his journal.

Mission 6 · Healthy Forest Missions

Two last crystal missions, plus a fun bonus craft to celebrate!

Finale · Review & Goodbye

You helped Bobo become healthy! 🎉

All 6 Health Crystals are restored — Bobo is strong, clean, energetic, and dancing with everyone!

legs, feet, toes, shoulders, knees, left, right, wash, brush, clean, strong, healthy, smile
