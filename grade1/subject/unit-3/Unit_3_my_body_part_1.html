Grade 1 · My Body

Hi, I'm Lumi! ✨

Welcome, little helper! Today we're going on a My Body adventure together!

My robot friend Bobo went to play in the Magic Playground... and a giant gust of wind blew all of his body parts away! Will you help me find them?

☀️ What's the weather like today?

📅 What day is it?

—
Chapter 1 · Warm-Up Game

Lumi hears mysterious rustling in the grass... "I think Bobo's head and hair are hiding under these grassy mounds! Tap each one to look!"

🔍 Flip the mounds — find HEAD and HAIR!

Tap a mound. If it's a body part, say it out loud with Lumi!

Chapter 2 · Listening & Speaking

We found Bobo's head and hair! Now Bobo wakes up, wants to chat with you, and needs your help practicing my and your — then it's time to move and speak for real!

Chapter 3 · Phonics Adventure

We travel into the Magic Forest to look for Bobo's nose and mouth! Three glowing doors block our path — unlock them with short vowel body words: hand, leg, neck, lip, chin.

Chapter 4 · Reading

Under a mossy rock, we find Bobo's little diary! Let's read it together to find Bobo's hands.

These are my hands.
I have two hands.
My hands have fingers.
These are my eyes.
This is my face.
I am happy.

Let's check! 📖

Chapter 5 · Writing

Only Bobo's legs are left! To finish the spell, Lumi needs your best handwriting magic.

Chapter 6 · Celebration Games

Bobo is whole again! 🎉 Now let's celebrate with some fun body-part games!

Finale · Review & Goodbye

You helped Bobo today! 🎉

Bobo has a head, hair, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, hands and legs again — all thanks to you!

🗣️ Vocabulary

head, hair, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, teeth, face, hands, fingers, arms, legs, feet, neck, chin

📚 Grammar

"These are my eyes." · "This is your nose." · "My hands are strong." · "Your eyes are beautiful." · My vs Your

🔤 Phonics

short a: hand · short e: leg, neck · short i: lip, chin
